Study Smarter, Not Longer!

Good study habits can make school feel more manageable. Try these strategies and discover which ones work best for you.

Six Ways to Improve Your Study Habits

⏰

1. Try the Pomodoro Method

Focus on one task for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. Repeat as needed.

🔁

2. Use Active Recall

Close your notes and quiz yourself. Explaining ideas from memory helps you find what you still need to review.

📆

3. Plan Ahead

Break large assignments into smaller steps and spread studying over several days.

🃏

4. Make Flashcards

Put a question on one side and the answer on the other. Try answering before flipping the card.

📵

5. Limit Distractions

Choose a quiet workspace, silence unnecessary notifications, and focus on one subject at a time.

💤

6. Make Time for Rest

Take breaks, get enough sleep, and give yourself time to recharge before a big test.

Before Your Next Test ✅

Make sure you understand the main concepts, practice without looking at your notes, review mistakes, and prepare your materials the night before.
